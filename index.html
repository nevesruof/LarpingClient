Built to stay out of your way.

The visuals are loud. The client isn't: it caps frames when you're not playing and caches what it can.

Smart FPS limits

Menus, AFK and alt-tab get their own frame cap. Your FPS limit in Options is never touched.

Cached 3D cosmetics

Wings, hats and auras rebuild their geometry up to 60 times a second and reuse it between frames, so high refresh rates stay smooth.

A HUD that snaps

Drag any element. It snaps to edges, the center and other elements, with a grid and 1 px arrow-key nudges.

Three profiles

Save your whole setup in three slots and swap between a PvP layout and a clean one in a click.

Discord presence

Your Discord status shows Larp Client, the server you're on and the game version, with two buttons you choose.

Your real hotbar keys

Each hotbar slot shows the key it's bound to, even if your binds are 1 2 3 4 G R.

22 hats, 8 wings, 11 auras.

Every cosmetic is real 3D geometry that moves with you. Wings flap as you walk, capes flow, auras orbit. These are actual renders from the client.

Installed in four steps.

About two minutes if you already have Minecraft 1.21.11.

1

Install Fabric

Run the Fabric installer for Minecraft 1.21.11.

2

Add the mods

Put the Larp Client jar you got from Take and Fabric API in your .minecraft/mods folder.

3

Launch Fabric

Pick the Fabric 1.21.11 profile in the Minecraft Launcher and press Play.

4

Open the menu

Press Right Shift or click Client on the title screen. Turn on what you like.

Visual only. On purpose.

Larp changes how the game looks, never how it plays. These are the things you won't find in it, and won't in future versions.

Questions people ask.

How do I get Larp Client?
Larp Client is private and still in development, so it isn't public yet. Message Take and you'll get access.
Is Larp Client allowed on servers?
Larp only changes what you see on your own screen. It has no combat, movement or auto-click modules, so it plays like any other visual client. Each server sets its own rules, so check theirs if you are unsure.
Can other players see my cosmetics?
No. Cosmetics are drawn on your screen only. Press F5 to see them on yourself.
How do I open the menu?
Press Right Shift in game, or click Client on the title screen. You can change the key in Options › Controls › Larp Client.
Does it work with Sodium and other performance mods?
Yes. Larp is made to run next to Sodium, Lithium and Entity Culling, and they are the first mods we recommend. With Iris shaderpacks, some 3D effects can look different depending on the pack.
Which Minecraft versions are supported?
Minecraft 1.21.11 with Fabric Loader 0.18.1 or newer, Fabric API and Java 21. Forge, NeoForge and older versions are not supported.
Where are my settings saved?
In your config folder: larpclient.json for settings, larpclient-profile-1.json to -3 for profiles, and larpclient-waypoints.json and larpclient-macros.json. Copy them to move your setup to another PC.
The Music Player doesn't show the album cover.
The Music Player reads Windows media controls, so it only works on Windows. If the cover still doesn't show, see Music player for the log file to check.
