Lyrics

Bring the words into your world.

Powered by LRCLIB#

Lyrics uses track information from the Windows media session to retrieve lyrics from LRCLIB. Availability and synchronized timing depend on the song’s metadata and what LRCLIB has for that recording.

Three placements#

  • World: place the current phrase in the world, fixed per line or following the player.
  • HUD: a movable lyrics widget.
  • Compact: a single-line style, including an option above the music player.

Make the text yours#

Adjust size, width, line layout, line spacing, color and animation speed. Font choices include Inter (iOS), Inter Bold (iOS), Montserrat and Minecraft. Text effects include Fade, Slide, Glow and None.

Timing and paused playback#

Use Sync offset in milliseconds to fine-tune the line timing. Hide when paused controls whether the lyric remains visible after playback stops. Unsynchronized lyrics do not provide automatic word-level karaoke timing.

No lyrics found?#

Check that the media session reports the right song and artist. Some songs, alternate recordings and instrumental tracks have no matching lyric entry.
