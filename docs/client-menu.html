DocsGetting started

The client menu

Open the menu, find mods, change their settings and bind keys.

Press Right Shift in game, or click Client on the title screen. The menu has a sidebar with six pages:

PageWhat's there
ModulesHUD, PvP and Utility mods, with tabs for each plus Enabled and Pinned.
CosmeticsCape, Wings, Hats, Aura and Trail, with a live preview.
VisualsWorld, combat and camera effects, with World, Combat, Camera and Enabled tabs.
WaypointsYour saved places. See Waypoints.
MacrosKeys that send a command or message. See Macros.
RPCYour Discord status and a preview of it. See Discord RPC.

At the bottom are Settings (theme, menus, profiles) and Edit HUD. The number next to each page is how many mods are on there.

Mod cards#

  • Click the switch or right-click the card to turn a mod on or off.
  • Click the card to open its settings.
  • Click the pin to keep a mod at the top, and in the Pinned tab.
  • Use the search button to find a mod by name or description.

Settings page#

Each mod's settings are grouped into sections, with checkboxes in two columns, sliders and option pickers. The back arrow returns to the list. Reset settings puts the mod back to how it came, including its HUD position.

Binding a key to a mod#

On a mod's settings page, click the key button and press any key. Right-click it to clear the bind. The key shows on the mod's card, and pressing it in game toggles the mod with a small notification.

Theme#

In Settings → Theme choose an accent color: Indigo, Orange, Purple, Blue, Red, Pink, Green, White. It colors switches, glows and the HUD. Menu background switches the title screen between fluid curtains and the blurred panorama.
