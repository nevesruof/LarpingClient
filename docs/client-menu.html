The client menu

Your modules, preferences and personal setup.

Open your setup#

Press Right Shift or choose Larp Client on the title screen. Browse HUD, PvP, Utility, Visuals and Cosmetics, or open the dedicated Kill Efects section.

What changed#

  • Emblem-focused home screen with a player preview, your last server and current music.
  • A shared dark line background in out-of-world menus; in a world, menus can leave the game visible behind them.
  • Custom accent selection and directly editable text fields.
  • Profile deletion, reset confirmation and animated notifications.

English and Spanish#

Hover the thin bar at the bottom of the title screen to reveal the language selector. Move away to close it. The client supports English and Spanish.

Changing settings#

Open a module’s options to adjust its behavior. NEW badges identify recent additions. You can use the search field to find a module quickly.
