DocsFeatures

Cosmetics

Actual renders of wings, hats, auras and markers.

Your own view of the world#

Cosmetics are rendered locally. Use third-person view to see your own character. Color, scale and behavior are adjustable in the client; available options depend on the cosmetic.

Wings#

Eight styles, including Dragon, Angel, Butterfly, Crystal and Phoenix.

Hats#

From antlers and ears to crowns and halos.

Auras#

Orbiting shapes, elemental effects and ground runes.

Target markers#

Target ESP is part of Visuals; its styles are shown here alongside the cosmetic renders.

Movement and flight#

Cosmetic attachment transforms follow the player’s body, including Elytra poses. Different effects have their own animation and refresh controls. See Performance to tune your setup.
