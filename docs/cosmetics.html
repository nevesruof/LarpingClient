DocsFeatures

Cosmetics

3D wings, hats, auras, capes and trails that move with you.

Cosmetics are real 3D models drawn around your player. Only you see them, on your own screen. Press F5 to look at yourself. Every cosmetic can use the theme accent, a fixed color, your own color or rainbow.

The images on this page are rendered with the same code the client uses.

Hats#

22 hats: crowns, halos, ears, horns, antlers and the China Hat. In Hats you can change the scale, height and opacity, and make it bob, spin or follow your head.

Wings#

8 styles. They flap faster as you move, and you can set the scale, height, opacity and flap speed in Wings.

Aura#

Aura has five modes: Orbiting (shards, swords, orbs, runes or rings circling you), Elemental (flame, frost, void, sakura or storm), Ground Runes, Energy Sphere and Lightning Arcs.

Target ESP#

A marker on the player you're fighting, from Target ESP. 9 styles. It's part of Visuals, not Cosmetics, but uses the same 3D engine.

Cape and Trail#

Cape is a flowing cape with your colors and the Larp logo, in Gradient, Solid or Split styles with physics you can tune. Trail leaves a ribbon of light behind you, with optional sparkles.

Performance#

Cosmetic shapes are rebuilt up to 60 times a second and reused between frames, so at 144 FPS or more most of the work is skipped. Change it in Settings → Performance → Cosmetic FPS (30, 60, 120 or Max).
