Changelog

What changed in each version of Larp Client.

0.9.5 · Performance and fixes#

  • 3D cosmetics, kill effects and target markers cache their geometry. New Cosmetic FPS setting (30, 60, 120, Max).
  • Faster module lookups and cached text and icon sizes in every menu.
  • Performance and FPS Boost no longer save their temporary values into your options while the Options screen is open.
  • Kill Effect only plays for your own kills.

0.9.4 · Visual refresh#

  • Polished visuals across the menu, module pages and sections.

0.9.3 · Hotbar Keybinds#

  • New Hotbar Keybinds: the key bound to each slot, shown on the hotbar and in inventories.
  • More HUD and PvP modules.

0.9.1 – 0.9.2 · Hitboxes and Discord#

  • New Hitboxes: clean custom hitboxes instead of F3+B.
  • Discord RPC shows Larp Client, the server and the game version, with a logo and no background.
  • The crown on the Info Bar sits on the R and is smaller.

0.9.0 · 3D cosmetics#

  • New cosmetics engine: 8 wing styles, 22 hats, auras, kill effects and target markers.
  • Your name and an underline above the title-screen character.
  • A crown on your name in the Info Bar.

0.8.0 · New menu#

  • New client menu with a sidebar, centered tabs, mod cards and full settings pages.
  • Separate Visuals and RPC pages, plus Settings and Edit HUD buttons.
  • Fluid curtains on the title screen and a clean loading screen.

0.7.x · Title screen#

  • New title screen with a small player on the left and a Client button.
  • Album covers in the Music Player load reliably.
