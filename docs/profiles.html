Profiles

Save your whole setup and switch in one click.

Go to Settings → Profiles in the client menu. There are three slots. Save stores every mod, setting, keybind and HUD position in a slot; Load brings it back.

Where it's stored#

config/larpclient.jsonYour current settings
config/larpclient-profile-1.json to -3Profiles
config/larpclient-waypoints.jsonWaypoints
config/larpclient-macros.jsonMacros

Copy these files to move your setup to another PC or instance.
