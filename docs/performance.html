Performance

Get the most FPS out of Larp Client.

What Larp does for you#

  • Performance caps your FPS in menus, while AFK and when the game isn't focused. It never touches the FPS limit in Options.
  • FPS Boost turns heavy video settings down (entity shadows, clouds, biome blend, entity distance, view bobbing) and restores your exact values when you turn it off.
  • Cosmetics are cached and rebuilt at a set rate (see Cosmetics).
  • Text widths, icons and module lookups are cached, and the menus draw in a few batches.

Recommended mods#

Put these in the same mods folder. All are on Modrinth; download the 1.21.11 Fabric version of each.

SodiumRewrites chunk rendering. The biggest FPS boost you can get.
LithiumSpeeds up physics, mob AI and ticking. Great in singleplayer and big farms.
Entity CullingSkips entities and block entities hidden behind walls.
FerriteCoreCuts memory use a lot, so fewer stutters from garbage collection.
ImmediatelyFastFaster HUD, text and entity rendering. Pairs well with Larp's HUD.
ModernFixFaster startup and lower memory use.
More CullingSkips block faces you can't see, like leaves.
Iris ShadersRuns shaderpacks on top of Sodium, if you want shaders.

Start with Sodium, Lithium and Entity Culling. They give most of the gain and rarely conflict with anything.

Video settings that matter#

  • Render distance costs the most. 10–12 chunks is plenty for PvP.
  • Simulation distance 5–8 for multiplayer.
  • Give Minecraft 4 GB of RAM. More doesn't make it faster and can cause longer pauses.
